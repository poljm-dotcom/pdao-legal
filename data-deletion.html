Overview

We respect your privacy and your right to request deletion of your personal data. This page explains how to request data deletion and what happens when you do.

What Data Can Be Deleted

You can request deletion of:

  • Your Facebook Page ID and associated metadata
  • Page Access Tokens
  • Historical notice content posted through the system
  • System logs associated with your account

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Send an email to pdaobauang@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Step 2: Include your Facebook Page ID or the email address associated with your account
  • Step 3: Clearly state that you are requesting deletion of all stored data
  • Step 4: We will confirm receipt and process your request within 5-7 business days

Alternative: Disconnect Your Account

You can immediately revoke access by disconnecting your Facebook Page from the PDAO system through the application settings. This will prevent future posting but does not automatically delete stored historical data. Please send an email to request permanent deletion of stored records.

Processing Timeline

We will process deletion requests within 5-7 business days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted. Please note that deletion of some data may take longer if required for legal compliance or system audits.

What Happens After Deletion

Once your data is deleted:

  • You will no longer be able to access the PDAO system with your Facebook Page
  • No new notices can be posted from your account
  • Historical logs may be retained for security purposes but will be anonymized
  • You can re-register at any time to use the system again

Contact Information

For data deletion requests or questions about this process, please contact:

Email: pdaobauang@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. If you have concerns about how your data is being handled, please contact us immediately.
