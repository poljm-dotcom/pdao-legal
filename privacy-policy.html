Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Introduction

This application ("the App") helps manage posts on your Facebook Page. We respect your privacy and describe below what information the App collects, why we collect it, how we use it, and how you can contact us or request deletion.

What we collect

  • Facebook authentication info: When you log in with Facebook we receive information necessary to authenticate and act on your Page (for example, a Page access token and your Page ID). We only request permissions required to post to and manage your Page.
  • Content you provide: Any post text, images, or configuration you create in the App in order to publish to your Page.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: Server logs, timestamps, and error reports used for debugging and to ensure service reliability.

How we use your data

  • To publish and schedule content to your Facebook Page as you instruct.
  • To authenticate and maintain the connection with your Page while you use the App.
  • To investigate and resolve technical issues, and to improve the App.

Data storage and retention

We store only the minimum data necessary for the App to function (for example, Page identifiers and access tokens). We retain this data only as long as required to provide the service or to comply with your requests (such as if you ask us to remove your Page connection).

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share data with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf (for example hosting or analytics), and only to the extent necessary. Where required by law, we may disclose information in response to lawful requests.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure — please contact us if you have concerns.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Facebook: You may disconnect your Facebook Page from the App at any time; this will revoke the App's access and stop future postings.
  • Data deletion: To request deletion of any stored data, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page: Data Deletion.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Contact

If you have questions or wish to request deletion, contact us at example@email.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes we will post the updated date at the top of the policy.
